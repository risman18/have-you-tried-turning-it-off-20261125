Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Have You Tried Turning It Off?"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-have-you-tried-turning-it-off-154a938b): one funny line a hopeless tech support agent says to a desperate customer.

## The line

> Great news, I found your problem — it's the Wi-Fi. Bad news, I just lost it again. Have you tried turning the router off, then on, then quietly thanking it?

## Why it wins

- **Cheerful uselessness is the joke.** The agent "solves" the problem and instantly loses it again — the hopelessness is in the agent, never in the customer.
- **Twists the classic.** It leans on "turn it off and on again" and then swerves: the final step is to *thank* the router, a deadpan escalation no stock line has.
- **Sounds like a real support call.** "Great news / Bad news" is the exact cadence of a tier-1 agent reading from a script while improvising badly, and the customer's desperation is felt, not explained.
- **Tight on first read.** 156 characters, one line, every word earns its place — the punchline lands before the reader has time to expect it.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, deterministic) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
